## 📖 Series Overview

*The Petkotkweyak Chronicles* is an epic multi-book survival saga following **Kwitn "Gwidn" Simon ("Mac")**, a practical Mi'kmaw carpenter, builder, and Kinap; **Maya Lin**, an ER trauma nurse and resourceful maker; and their companions **Nipn** (Eastern timber wolf), **Wojkw** (Red Fox living neck warmer), and **The Fundy Black Bear** (honorary shoreline guard). 

Set in the tidal watershed of the Petkotkweyak (Petitcodiac River), New Brunswick (*Siknikt District of Mi'kma'ki*), the story documents a 20-year transition through societal collapse without Hollywood tropes, focusing on real physics, bio-hazard discipline, traditional ecological knowledge (*Netukulimk*), and grounded human connection.

## 🐺 Core Principles
1. **Grounded Dialogue:** Authentic, natural Maritime rhythms and wit—zero artificial technobabble in dialogue.
2. **Netukulimk:** Taking only what is needed, giving back to the land, and honoring natural systems.
3. **Hard Physics & Craft:** True caloric math, mechanical advantage, biological safety, and honest hand labor.
