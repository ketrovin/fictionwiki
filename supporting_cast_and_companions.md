# Supporting Cast & Companion Dossier
**Setting:** The Creek Camp & The Old Stone Light, Petkotkweyak (*Siknikt*)  
**Core Household:** Kwitn "Gwidn" Simon (Lead), Maya (Partner & Polymath), Chris (Cousin & Metalworker), Auntie Elizabeth (Elder & Anchor), and Nipn (Silent Watcher).

---

## 1. Maya (The Versatile Partner & Generalist)
* **Background:** Asian-Canadian, raised in a practical, resourceful household with a diverse spread of life skills and a DIY, problem-solving mindset.
* **Relationship Origin:** **Complete strangers pre-collapse.** They had never met before the grid died. Their connection begins post-collapse in early summer when their paths cross along the tidal river trails—built on mutual observation, respect for each other's calm competence, and earned trust.
* **Skill Philosophy (The Generalist):** She isn't pigeonholed into a single narrow specialty. Like Kwitn, she is **broadly capable, observant, and adaptable**:
  * **Food & Harvest:** Comfortable with lacto-fermentation, pickling, smoking, coastal seaweed foraging (dulse/kelp), and cultivating garden beds.
  * **Mending & Making:** Skilled with sewing, leather repair, canvas patching, net mending, and natural fiber cordage.
  * **First Aid & Health (The Living Pharmacopoeia):** Practical first-aid, wound hygiene, herbal teas, and burn/cut care. Carries a handmade **birchbark pocket medical ledger**, constantly interviewing Kwitn and Auntie Elizabeth to master every traditional plant remedy and map it to clinical pharmacology.
  * **Camp Logistics & Defense:** Confident with hand tools, bow work, and basic perimeter watch.
* **Dynamic with the MC:** Two versatile, practical minds working in sync. They share chores naturally, trade book and life knowledge, and balance each other's ideas with dry humor and mutual respect.
  * **Her Nickname for Kwitn:** **"Mac"** (born from Maya telling him: *"You remind me of an old-school machinist who can build an airplane out of a tractor engine and two files. Measure twice, never panic, hates cheap zinc screws."* Short, snappy, and used with affectionate dry humor as their bond deepens).
  * **The Bushcraft Learning Curve:** For traditional wilderness craft (spruce-root lashing, crooked-knife pulls, pine-pitch epoxy, reading tidal silt), Maya has to ask Kwitn how it works. But once he explains the mechanics and demonstrates the cut once, her sharp intellect and manual dexterity take over—she masters it immediately.
  * **Attraction & The L'nu Honor Code:** Kwitn is fully aware of how attractive Maya is, but extracting sexual favors or using shelter/food as leverage is an unforgivable violation of L'nu honor and hospitality. His absolute decency allows real, mutual attraction to grow naturally from earned trust rather than coercion.

## 2. Chris (The Pragmatic Cousin)
* **Identity:** Mi'kmaw (L'nu), lifelong cousin and friend of the protagonist.
* **Background & Skills:** Worked in small-engine repair, welding, and metal salvage in town.
* **Role in Camp:** 
  * Manages the charcoal forge, reshapes scrap steel into tool heads, and reinforces gate hinges and iron fittings.
  * The physical heavy lifter and direct counter-voice to the MC's theoretical book planning.
* **Voice:** Sarcastic, affectionate, deeply loyal, and grounded in common sense.

---

## 3. Auntie Elizabeth / Kji-Kiju (The Elder Anchor)
* **Identity:** Mi'kmaw elder from the community.
* **Role in Camp:** 
  * **The Emotional & Cultural Hearth:** Keeps everyone grounded; brings historical perspective and oral storytelling to long winter nights.
  * **Botany & Seed Keeping:** Traditional plant lore (*Netukulimk*), heirloom seed saving, drying medicines (chaga, sweetgrass, sweet flag).
  * **Peacekeeper:** Ensures the household runs on mutual respect and patience.

---

## 4. Nipn ("Summer" — The Silent Watcher)
* **Identity:** Eastern Timber Wolf (*Canis lupus lycaon*) raised by Kwitn from a pup.
* **Origin & Backstory:**
  * Three years before the collapse, while timber-cruising deep in the remote crown hardwoods near the Miramichi watershed after an illegal trap-line cull, Kwitn found an orphaned gray-and-silver wolf pup whose den had been destroyed.
  * Kwitn took the pup in, bottle-fed him, and raised him with calm, firm respect for his wild nature.
  * He named him **Nipn** (Mi'kmaw for *"Summer"*). Nipn grew into a massive, quiet, intelligent wolf whose bond with Kwitn is built on mutual trust and silent understanding.
* **Role:** Scent tracker, perimeter early-warning alarm, and faithful hearth companion.
* **Discipline:** Pure silent sentry. Wolves naturally do not bark like domestic dogs; Nipn communicates through sharp ear pricks, body posture shifts, low nose nudges, and deep chest rumbles, scenting rotten wanderers up to half a kilometer downwind through the black spruce.
* **Household Structure:**
  * The Creek Camp / Stone Light is the quiet home of **Kwitn ("Mac"), Maya, Nipn (Timber Wolf), The Fundy Black Bear, and Wojkw (Red Fox)**.
  * Chris and Auntie Elizabeth are stationed downriver at the Fort Folly community hub, linked by the Clacks optical telegraph line and the rail draisine.

## 5. The Fundy Black Bear (The "Beggar Bear" / Honorary Shoreline Guard)
* **Identity:** A massive, 450-pound wild Fundy black bear. 
  * *Gender Mystery & Privacy:* Is it male or female? Mac refuses to check: *"I am not bending down with a lantern to inspect a four-hundred-pound bear's undercarriage. If the bear wants us to know its business, it can leave a note on the porch. Its name is Bear."*
* **Origin:** Met at the abandoned coastal rail-ferry wharf on Day 58 while Mac and Maya were jigging mackerel. Mac spent half a day tossing fresh fish to the patient bear.
* **Homestead Dynamic & The Shoreline Patrol:**
  * Followed the railway spur seven miles back to the Creek Camp. Recognizing a safe, peaceful, zombie-free sanctuary, it behaves itself, never trampling the garden mounds or touching crops.
  * Settled on the sunny gravel shelf by the fish weirs—the camp's previously unfenced mudflat flank. Any waterlogged walker drifting up out of the river silt gets casually swatted and crushed into the clay by a single 450-pound bear paw. In return, Mac tosses it fresh bass and shad scraps from the weirs.
* **The Bond with Maya (The "Puppy Bear"):**
  * When heavy, iron-rich Fundy mud bakes into painful clay balls between the bear's footpads and claws, Maya treats it with a warm chamomile-water soak, soft bristle brush, and spruce-pitch salve. 
  * The giant beast lays its dinner-plate paw across her knees with deep rumbling groans of relief, completely adoring Maya.
  * **Follows Her Like a Polite 450-lb Puppy:** It ambles five paces behind Maya wherever she goes in the yard—waiting patiently while she harvests runner beans, sitting on its haunches when she stops to hang linen, and nudging her elbow with a cold, wet nose. Nipn and the Bear maintain an easy, peaceful truce: Nipn runs the deep ridges and outer trails, while the Bear is Maya's devoted, heavyweight garden escort.

## 6. Wojkw ("Fox" / Mac's Living Neck Warmer)
* **Identity:** A fiery, intelligent Maritime red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*).
* **Origin & Rescue:** Cornered inside a hollow cedar deadfall root-wad by two walkers along the upper ridge trail. Mac stepped in and silently impaled both deadheads with his ash bear-spear, freeing the trapped fox and tossing it a sliver of dried smoked mackerel.
* **The "Living Fur Collar" Habit:** 
  * Instead of fleeing into the brush, the fox hopped onto Mac’s broad shoulders and draped itself completely around the back of his neck like a luxuriant, fiery-red living fur scarf. 
  * It curls its chin on Mac's collarbone, drapes its bushy white-tipped tail across his left shoulder, and falls asleep purring/churring softly.
* **Mac's Softie Reaction ("Aww, look at you..."):** Despite his massive Kinap frame, Mac is a complete softie at heart when it comes to animals. When the fox curls around his neck, he totally melts: *"Aww, look at you... you're adorable."* He happily lets it ride on his shoulders all day, gently scratching its ears while he works.
* **Homestead Harmony:** Completely peaceful with Nipn, Maya, and the Bear; rides Mac’s neck everywhere he goes like a warm, purring little friend.
