# Author's Lore Bible: The Infected (Solanum-Romero Mechanics)
**Classification:** Canonical Virus Mechanics (*Solanum-Romero Hybrid*)  
**Visibility:** **Author Knowledge Only** (The protagonist observes the symptoms and mechanics, but does not know the clinical origin or name).  
**Core Inspiration:** George A. Romero classic rules + Max Brooks (*The Zombie Survival Guide / World War Z*) biological physics.

---

## 1. Transmission & Infection Vector

```
[ Bite / Fluid Contact ] ──▶ [ Rapid Neurotoxin / Fever ] ──▶ [ Clinical Death (12-24 hrs) ] ──▶ [ Brainstem Reanimation ]
```

* **Vector:** Strictly fluid-borne (saliva, black coagulated blood, spinal fluid) entering an open wound, bite, or mucous membrane (eyes, mouth, nasal passages).
* **Non-Airborne / Non-Waterborne:** The virus cannot survive in open air or water currents. Water sources remain safe if boiled/filtered against standard bacteria.
* **Lethality:** 100% fatal once fluid enters the bloodstream. No natural human immunity.
* **Reanimation Timeline:**
  * Hour 0–5: High fever ($40^\circ\text{C}+$ / $104^\circ\text{F}$), shivering, severe joint pain.
  * Hour 6–12: Coma, progressive cardiac arrest, clinical brain death.
  * Hour 12–24: Cessation of all metabolic life, followed by immediate viral re-ignition of the primitive **brainstem and cerebellum**.

## 2. Physiology & Anatomy (How They Work)

* **The "Black Sludge" (Coagulated Fluid):**
  * The heart does not beat. Blood does not circulate. Within hours of death, circulatory fluids thicken into a dark, viscous, foul-smelling black gel. 
  * Wounds do not bleed out; blood does not spray under pressure (eliminating arterial spray, but making splatter from blunt trauma a biohazard).
* **Zero Cellular Repair:**
  * Scrapes, broken bones, and torn muscles never heal. A broken ankle remains permanently broken, forcing the zombie to drag itself forever.
* **Toxicity to Microorganisms & Carrion Scavengers:**
  * Why don't they rot away in two weeks like normal corpses? **The Solanum virus produces an enzyme toxic to standard terrestrial bacteria and carrion predators.**
  * Microbes consume them at a much slower rate. However, blowflies, maggots, and extreme maritime weather (freeze-thaw) still physically degrade the tissue over months and years.
* **Pain & Fatigue:**
  * Zero pain reception. Zero lactic acid buildup or muscle fatigue. They move with 100% of their remaining muscle fibers until the tendon physically snaps or tears free from the bone.

## 4. The Kill Mechanism: Why the Open-Mouth Spear Works

* **The Only Off-Switch:** Complete physical destruction of the **brainstem and cerebellum** (located at the lower rear of the cranial vault).
* **The Skull Paradox:** The human cranium is an arched dome of dense bone designed to deflect blunt impacts and piercing thrusts.
* **The Soft Palate Highway:** When lunging to bite, the zombie opens its jaw wide. The roof of the mouth consists of thin membrane and porous bone, sitting **directly beneath the brainstem and C1/C2 cervical junction**.
* **Result:** A spear guided through the open mouth punches directly into the brainstem or severs the spinal cord with zero deflection and minimal required force.

## 5. Environmental Interactions (The Maritime Terrain)

### 1. Water & The 30-Foot Fundy Tides
* **They Cannot Swim:** Lacking lungs with air displacement and motor coordination, zombies **sink straight to the bottom like stones.**
* **The Mud Silt Trap:** On the red clay mudflats of Petkotkweyak, an infected sinks past its knees within paces. When the tidal bore surges in, the dead are pinned, submerged, and swept into the bay by current friction.
* **Submerged Dead:** Zombies can survive indefinitely underwater (no oxygen requirement). Rivers, tidal channels, and flooded cellars harbor silent, stationary "bottom-walkers."

### 2. The Winter Freeze ($-15^\circ\text{C}$ to $-25^\circ\text{C}$)
* **The Ice Crystal Effect:** With zero metabolic heat, bodily fluids crystallize into solid ice.
* **Complete Paralysis:** From December through March, outdoor zombies are frozen completely rigid.
* **Cellular Rupture:** Ice expansion fractures cell walls and degrades tendon elasticity. Every spring thaw leaves surviving infected slower, stiffer, and mechanically weaker than the year before.
