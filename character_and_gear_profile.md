# Character Dossier & Gear Inventory
**Name:** Kwitn "Gwidn" Simon  
**Pronunciation & Meaning:** *Gwee-dn* (L'nu for "Birchbark Canoe" / Vessel on Water)  
**Identity:** Mi'kmaw (L'nu), native to the Siknikt District (*Chignecto / Simon lineage*)  
**Core Archetype:** The Practical Bookworm / Traditional Maker  
**Philosophy:** *"Common sense is a lot rarer than brass. Work with the water, let physics do the killing, and keep your socks dry."*

---

## 1. Character Profile & Psychology

### Background & Mindset
* **Pre-Collapse Life:** Had begun transitioning to an off-grid lifestyle along a quiet river tributary outside town. Was actively building a timber cabin, clearing garden beds, testing tidal fish weirs, and collecting hand tools and physical books.
* **The Canoe Rationale (The Name & The Craft):** His name is literally *Kwitn* ("Canoe" in L'nui'simk). He made a deliberate point years ago to learn how to build, steam, and paddle traditional cedar-rib canoes because, in his own words: *"If your name is Canoe and you live five miles from a tidal river in Mi'kma'ki and you don't know how to build a boat, you’re basically volunteering to be the punchline of a very long cosmic joke."*
* **The "Smart, Not High-IQ" Distinction:** He is not a pompous academic. His intelligence is grounded in **pattern recognition, practical physics, and common sense** (*what Terry Pratchett calls "Horse Sense" or "Headology"*).
* **The Living Map:** His primary survival asset is internal. He carries a mental, breathing map of Siknikt that cross-references ancestral waterways, topography, vegetation, fiction tropes, and real-time sensory observation.
* **Voice & Temperament:** Dry, observant, unflappable, with a quiet maritime humor. He does not panic or seek heroics; he treats survival as a set of logical household chores.
* **The Kinap Nature (*Gjinap* / Underspoken Titan):** In Mi'kmaw oral tradition, a *Kinap* is someone blessed with extraordinary, deep physical strength, abnormal endurance, and quiet vitality. True to tradition, a real Kinap never boasts, postures, or shows off. Mac never puffs his chest or acts like a movie strongman; he casually hoists green 300-pound oak logs, levers multi-ton boulders, cranks railway handcars for six hours uphill without losing his breath, and holds heavy iron gates shut against surging water with an easy, relaxed posture. If anyone notices, he just shrugs and mumbles about *"letting the leverage do the work."*
* **The Ironwood Hide (Un-bite-able Skin — Unknown to Him):** True Kinap physiology carries an innate, stone-dense dermal resilience (like petrified birch or Fundy basalt). However, **Mac does not know this**. Because he is so ruthlessly disciplined, cautious, and methodical—always wearing wax-hardened leather bracers, thick Mackinaw wool, keeping six feet of distance with his bear-spear, and never letting anything close—he has never actually been bitten on bare skin. If an infected ever somehow clamped bare jaws onto his arm, its teeth would instantly shatter into powder like biting a granite boulder. His immediate, bewildered reaction wouldn't be heroic grandeur; he'd just stare at his intact skin, look at the toothless corpse, and yell: *"What the fuck??? Hey Maya, come see this!"*

## 3. Weapons & Combat Doctrine

He strictly avoids firearms (which he views as noisy "dinner bells" that attract hordes and depend on finite ammunition) in favor of quiet, durable, handcrafted weapons:

```
[ Ash Bear-Spear (6 ft) ] ──▶ Braced against ground ──▶ Enters open mouth ──▶ Severs brainstem / C1-C2 spine
[ Ash Self-Bow (48 lb) ]  ──▶ Hand-fletched cedar   ──▶ Silent, reusable ──▶ Small game & perimeter control
```

### 1. The Heavy Ash Bear-Spear (Primary Weapon & Anti-Blood Engineering)
* **Shaft:** 6-foot seasoned white ash, hand-carved with a crooked knife for optimal balance and straight grain alignment.
* **Head:** Hand-ground high-carbon steel leaf blade (nine-inch 5160 leaf spring steel).
* **The Boar/Bear Crossguard:** A thick 5-inch steel crossbar welded behind the socket (or hardwood lug lashed with rawhide). Stops an impaled corpse six feet away, preventing it from sliding down the shaft.
* **The Conical Fluid Deflector (Blood Guard Collar):** A 4-inch flared conical collar of hammered sheet copper/hardened leather mounted directly behind the crossguard. When infected blood spurts or drains down the blade, the conical flange deflects the fluid outward onto the dirt, preventing dangerous fluids from tracking down the pole onto the defender's hands.
* **The Raised Pitch-Dam (Capillary Break):** A braided hemp ring soaked in boiled spruce-tar epoxy at the 2-foot mark, acting as a physical dam to stop creeping surface moisture.
* **Hydrophobic Forward Shaft:** The front 24 inches of the ash pole are saturated with hot beeswax and bear tallow so infectious fluid cannot soak into the porous wood fibers.
* **Tactical Application:** Never thrusts with arm strength. He plants the butt against a bedrock notch or root, levels the point at jaw-height, and lets the charging zombie drive its open mouth onto the blade, punching through the soft palate into the brainstem while all splatter falls six feet away.

### 2. The Traditional Ash Hunting Bow & Arrows
* **Bow:** 48-pound draw ash flatbow, backed with sinew, sealed with pine pitch.
* **Ammunition:** Port Orford cedar and willow shafts, fletched with wild goose feathers, tipped with hand-filed scrap steel and bone points. Completely silent, 100% reusable.

## 4. Tool Discipline & Anti-Contamination Protocols ("Not an Idiot")

In zombie fiction, characters constantly do stupid things like stabbing an infected corpse and then using the same blade to slice an apple or dress a deer. He understands basic biology, germ theory, and bloodborne pathogens:

* **Strict Arrow Segregation:**
  * **White/Gray Fletched Arrows (The Clean Quiver):** Strictly reserved for live game (deer, geese, hares). Kept sealed in a grease-treated leather tube.
  * **Black/Charred Fletched Arrows (The "Hot" Quiver):** Dedicated exclusively for infected defense. Once an arrow pierces a dead corpse, it is never used for food harvesting, even if washed.
* **Blade Separation:**
  * **The Spear & Defense Chopper:** Weapons that contact infected fluids never touch food, cooking pots, or clean woodcraft.
  * **The Crooked Knife & Butchering Blades:** Kept in separate waxed sheaths, wiped with boiled spruce pitch/mineral oil, and strictly reserved for carving and game prep.
* **Splatter & Fluid Discipline:**
  * Avoids close-quarter swinging weapons (like baseball bats or machetes) that spray contaminated black fluid into the eyes, nose, or mouth.
  * The 6-foot spear keeps fluid splatter six feet away at all times.

He rejects modern 60-pound "tacticool" backpacks. His loadout is compact, silent, and field-maintainable:

* **The Crooked Knife (*Mokotaugan* / *Pkwe'kn*):** His primary crafting tool. Pulled toward the body with one hand, used for shaping wood, carving staves, building weirs, and making snowshoes.
* **Whetstones & Files:** Fine-grit natural river stones and a 6-inch mill bastard file for maintaining razor edges on axes, knives, and spears.
* **Cordage & Pitch Tin:** Hand-twisted cedar bark cordage, bank line, and a tin of melted spruce resin mixed with charcoal dust (nature's waterproof epoxy).
* **Fire Kit:** Strike-a-light flint and high-carbon steel striker, chaga fungus (*tinder polypore*), and birchbark strips stored in a waxed pouch.

## 5. Clothing & Footwear

Built for silent movement and damp maritime weather:

* **Footwear:** Oil-tanned, double-soled moose-hide/cowhide moccasins for silent trail walking. Felted wool liner boots for late autumn and winter. Zero pavement-clomping noise.
* **Outer Layers:** Heavy Mackinaw wool jacket (remains warm when wet, silent against brush, immune to campfire spark burns) and tightly woven canvas pants.
* **The Living Neck Warmer (*Wojkw* the Red Fox):** Rescued from walkers, a clever red fox that permanently drapes itself around Mac's neck and shoulders like a thick, heated fur stole. Mac is a complete softie for animals and totally melted: *"Aww, look at you... you're so cute."* He happily carries the purring fox around all day, gently scratching its ears while working.
* **Forearm & Shin Guards:** Layered, wax-hardened leather strapped over wool to provide 100% bite-proof protection against sudden grabs in close brush.

## 6. Homestead & Infrastructure Assets

* **Cabin:** Low-profile, half-timbered camp nestled under mature hemlock canopy along a freshwater tributary feeding into the Petkotkweyak.
* **The Food Engine:**
  * **3 Shallow Pit Weirs:** Woven alder and stone funnels that drop trapped flounder, shad, and bass into deep, cool retention pools during low tide, keeping food alive until needed.
  * **Root Cellar:** Hand-dug, stone-lined subterranean storage insulated with dry cedar shavings and sphagnum moss.
  * **Woodpile:** 8 cords of seasoned sugar maple, yellow birch, and ash, stacked bark-up under birchbark tarps.
