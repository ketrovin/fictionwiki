## 1. Mac — The Kinap

### Core Nature & Spiritual Anatomy
* **Identity & True Name:** An ex-archivist, bibliophile, and scholar who retreated to the red silt banks of the Petitcodiac (*Petkotkweyak*) a decade before the collapse. He carries the quiet, ancient mantle of a **Kinap** (a Mi'kmaw mythical titan of immense, grounded power).
  * **His True Name:** His true name in the L'nu language is ***Kwitn***—the traditional ocean-going birchbark canoe with raised gunwales that can ride forty-foot Fundy breakers without sinking. Because English and French speakers stumble over the phonemes and cannot pronounce it without twisting their tongues, he simply goes by **Mac**.
* **The Situational Strength (Unteachable):**
  * Mac is as strong as he *wants* or *needs* to be in any given moment.
  * There are no bulging muscles, no theatrical energy auras, no grunting wind-ups. Whether lifting a 12-lb bag of meal, an 800-lb granite hearthstone, or wedging a runaway spruce log against a rushing tidal bore, his body answers his will with calm, effortless poise.
  * It is entirely inborn and **cannot be taught** to anyone else. To observers, it looks strangely casual—as if gravity simply forgot to pull down on whatever Mac is holding.
* **Situational Invulnerability (Bulletproof by Will):**
  * Mac is **completely bulletproof** if he wants to be.
  * That desire and density take effect instantaneously—as quickly as the sound of a gunshot reaches his ears.
  * Jacketed rifle rounds, heavy ballistics, buckshot, and shrapnel simply flatten, shatter, or deflect off his skin and clothing like hail hitting Mary's Point granite, dropping spent into the mud.
  * There are no glowing energy shields, visual auras, or metallic skin effects; he remains a normal-sized, weathered woodsman in a wool shirt and moose-hide vest whose flesh and covenant with the earth simply refuse ballistic entry.
* **Channeling Mntu (The Azure Gaze):**
  * In Mi'kmaw spiritual cosmology, *Mntu* (the primordial spiritual power and vital energy of creation) flows through the living world. As a *Kinap*, Mac can channel *Mntu* directly from the bedrock, the deep salt tide, and the ancient soil.
  * In daily life, his eyes are quiet, weathered, and unassuming.
  * But when he is **heavily channeling *Mntu***—whether anchoring himself against the crushing force of a tidal wave, withstanding heavy automatic fire, executing thirty-foot Kinap leaps, or entering lethal combat—the unmistakable **azure blue** flares visibly in his eyes.
  * It is not a flashy comic-book effect, but a deep, piercing incandescence, like cold winter sunlight refracted through fifty feet of glacial sea ice or an electric arc deep underwater. To an enemy caught staring into his face, that azure gaze is the silent, terrifying realization that they are facing a sovereign titan of the red river.
* **The Quiet Lethality (How Dangerous Mac Is):**
  * **Core Ethos:** Mac prefers peace. He wants to garden, boil sap, read Johnson and Pratchett, forge iron, feed his animals, and watch the tide. He avoids violence wherever possible because violence is a waste of energy, life, and the land's harmony.
  * **The Combat Cadence (Peaceful Arrival -> The Inhale -> The Gaze -> Absolute Violence):**
    1. *The Peaceful Arrival:* Mac always shows up peaceful, loose, and unassuming. Hands open or resting on a cedar staff, posture relaxed, voice quiet. He gives men every opportunity to turn their boats around and leave the river in one piece.
    2. *The Line Crossed:* The moment it goes bad—an intruder racks a bolt, raises a rifle, laughs at a warning, or makes a move toward Maya or the homestead.
    3. *The Inhale:* Mac goes dead silent. He draws a single, deep, measured breath through his nose, pulling *Mntu* straight out of the red bedrock and the salt wind into his lungs and sinew.
    4. *The Look Up:* His head lifts, locking eyes directly with the target.
    5. *The Azure Ignition:* The deep, incandescent azure blue ignites in his irises—cold, subterranean fire blazing in the dark.
    6. *Violence:* Instantaneous, terrifying, unstoppable finality. Bulletproof density locks in, physical force multiplies, and the threat is ended without hesitation or wasted motion.
  * **The Inevitable End (Absolute Lethality Against Killing Intent):**
    * *If you bring killing intent to the headland, you do not live.* No hesitation, no posturing, no theatrical movie one-liners, and no half-measures. Anyone who faces Mac with intent to murder Maya, slaughter his animals, or butcher his hearth is ended with cold, terrifying finality. He does not keep prisoners of war who came to kill.
    * **The Post-Battle Exhaustion & The Caregiver Role-Reversal:**
      * Channeling exponential *Mntu* through the high-speed figure-eight onslaught generates immense internal thermal and vascular friction. When the battle ends and the killers are dead, Mac is **deeply, bone-wearily exhausted**—his eyes dim, his forearms steam, and his titan frame needs rest.
      * This triggers the pivotal romantic and sovereign role-reversal: **it is Maya's turn to take care of him**. Battered though she is, Maya steps up to baby him—tending his frost-scorched knuckles with warm water and herbal salves, tucking him into dry wool blankets by the thermal hearth, feeding him rich hot broth, and fiercely guarding the homestead while the titan rests. Scar curls up on his chest like an eight-ounce furry hot-water bottle.
  * Combined with an intelligence network of every bird, wolf, and beast within ten miles, ambushing him is impossible.
* **Animal Communion (Teachable):**
  * True mutual comprehension. Mac speaks in voice, posture, and intent, and animals understand him completely. Conversely, he hears and comprehends their thoughts, instincts, territorial disputes, and perspectives.
  * **This can be taught.** It is a discipline of shedding deceit, tuning mental frequency, reading the grammar of breath and scent, and respecting *Netukulimk* (the sacred balance). He will eventually mentor Maya in this art.
* **Minor Energy Manipulation:**
  * Subtle, tactile movement of warmth and vitality.
  * Mac can coax warmth into frostbitten fingers, calm a racing heart with a touch to the chest, settle the heat of a stone hearth, or guide the cellular vitality of living wood to weave and heal.
* **Voice & Mind (Philology & Literary Wit):**
  * Deeply well-read. He peppers his thoughts and deadpan observations with etymological roots, Latin/Greek puns, and classical allusions (*kynikos* for Nipn, *robur* for oak timber, *skiouros* for squirrels, *Petkotkweyak* vs. *Petitcodiac*).
  * Devoted reader of Terry Pratchett (especially *Guards! Guards!* and the Boots Theory), Defoe, Roman agricultural manuals (Cato, Columella), and 19th-century Maritime trade logs.
  * His tone is warm, wry, self-amused, and thoroughly Maritime. Zero academic lecturing in dialogue—only dry, affectionate, unpretentious wit.

## 2. The 10-Year Homestead (Stealth & Utility)

Mac has spent ten patient years establishing this sanctuary on the high ridge overlooking the Petitcodiac bends near Belliveau Village. Nothing is built to show off; everything is disguised as natural growth or weathered Acadian ruins.

```
       [ FUNDY / PETITCODIAC RED MUDFLATS ]
                    |
          [ Camouflaged Tidal Weirs ]
     (Woven brush & stakes; catches shad/flounder)
                    |
        +-----------+-----------+
        |                       |
[ The Stone Lighthouse ]   [ The 2-Room Cabin ]
(Winter sanctuary,         (Spring-Autumn base,
 granite fortress,         low-slung cedar,
 spiral stair library)      deep thermal hearth)
        |                       |
        +-----------+-----------+
                    |
      [ Living-Wood Palisade Fence ]
  (Braided willow/cedar/hawthorn; self-healing,
   with heavy counterweighted tamarack gate)
                    |
        [ Elevated Bog Boardwalk ]
  (Split-cedar walkway: cattail starch,
   cloudberries/bakeapples, medicinal moss)
```

### A. The Two-Room Cabin (Spring to Autumn Base)
* **Construction:** Low-profile, hand-hewn sugar-maple and red spruce frame clad in split cedar shakes weathered to coastal drift-gray. Sits nestled into the leeward bank of black spruce.
* **Room 1 (The Common Room & Hearth):** Massive fieldstone fireplace backed by a two-ton dry clay-and-river-stone thermal battery. Holds heat for days. Workbench, hanging dried herbs, salt-pork racks, hand tools on pegs, and deep cedar shelving.
* **Room 2 (Sleeping Quarters & Pantry):** Insulated with wild sheep's wool and cattail down. Double bunk frames, secondary recovery cot, airtight zinc grain and flour casks, tool chests, seed stores.
* **Stealth Chimney Baffle:** Flue directs smoke down through an underground stone-and-gravel trench, cooling and filtering soot so the exhaust disperses at tree-canopy level as thin, odorless coastal mist.

### B. The Living-Wood Fence & Tamarack Gate
* Using his minor energy manipulation over ten years, Mac trained living black willow, thorny hawthorn, and coastal white cedar saplings to cross-braid and fuse their trunks as they grew.
* **The Palisade:** An eight-foot-tall, living, rooted, self-repairing wall that bends before hurricane-force gales without snapping. Completely impassable to intruders or large predators.
* **The Gate:** Heavy, hand-planed tamarack (hackmatack) hung on greased forged iron strap hinges. Perfectly balanced on a counterweighted stone pivot; glides shut with a quiet, solid click.

### C. The Bog Boardwalk
* Elevated split-cedar stringer walkway winding into the acidic peat bog behind the homestead.
* Prevents sinking into waist-deep quagmire while providing year-round access to:
  * **Cattails (*Typha*):** Starch flour from rhizomes, early shoots as greens, fluffy down for quilts and insulation.
  * **Cloudberries (Bakeapples):** Rare amber bog gold, loaded with Vitamin C.
  * **Bog Cranberries & Labrador Tea:** Cold-weather greens and tonic infusions.
  * **Sphagnum Moss:** Sterile, highly absorbent natural wound dressing that never rots.

### D. The Tidal Weirs (The Silt Traps)
* Sturdy brush-and-hurdle weirs staked into the red mud flats where the 40-foot Fundy tide recedes twice a day.
* Built to resemble random drift jams from the river.
* On the dropping tide, funnel flounder, gaspereau, and shad into a stone-lined catching pool, offering fresh protein twice daily without hooks or nets.

### E. The Stone Lighthouse (Winter Fortress & Tower Library)
* **Status:** Legally deeded to Mac before the collapse; sits on the headland overlooking Shepody Bay and the river bends.
* **First-Tier Keeper's Apartment:**
  * Mac’s **winter home**. Built into the massive four-foot-thick dressed granite base of the tower.
  * Heavily shuttered iron embrasures, heavy cast-iron cooking range, and deep stone thermal mass. Completely immune to Fundy nor'easters, freezing spray, and river ice jams.
* **The Curving Stair Library & The Rare Book Vault:**
  * The interior spiral stone staircase leading up the tower is lined on the outer curving wall with custom-fitted seasoned timber bookshelves.
  * Hundreds of dry, preserved volumes: classical Latin/Greek texts, botanical encyclopedias, civil engineering folios, OED lexicons, and Terry Pratchett paperbacks.
  * **The Antiquarian Vault (Rare Books & Folios):**
    * Mac spent decades as an archivist and collector. His lighthouse preserves the last great antiquarian collection in eastern Canada, stored in zinc-lined cedar chests and dry stone niches:
      * **Samuel Johnson’s *Dictionary of the English Language* (1755 First Edition, 2 folio volumes in full calfskin).**
      * **Champlain’s *Voyages* (1613/1632 edition)** with original folding copperplate maps of the Bay of Fundy (*Baie Françoise*) and the red river mouths.
      * **1730s Acadian Dyke Journals:** Hand-scribed notes detailing *aboiteau* construction (one-way tidal wooden clapper valves) and salt-marsh engineering.
      * **Gerard’s *Herball* (1633)** and Vitruvius’s *De Architectura* (1567 Venice edition) on hydraulic lime mortar and arches.
      * **Newton's *Principia* (1726 edition).**
    * Using his subtle energy manipulation, Mac keeps the stairwell at a perfect, stable microclimate (45% humidity, zero mold or salt damp), ensuring these physical survivors of human civilization outlast the collapse by centuries.
* **Upper Lantern Gallery & Watchroom:**
  * 360-degree glassed lantern room with brass naval telescope. Unbroken 20-mile surveillance over river channels, highways, and forest lines. Optical shutter signal station.

## 3. The Animal Companions & Cast

### **Nipn ("Summer") — Eastern Timber Wolf**
* Massive, silver-grey wolf with amber eyes. Mac’s primary scout, hunting partner, and intellectual equal in wilderness logic.
* Highly dignified, observant, and possessing dry comedic timing in his communication with Mac. Tolerates small creatures with stoic patience.

### **The Fundy Bear — Shoreline Guard & Beggar Bear**
* 450-lb Atlantic black bear. Befriended by Mac through fish scraps, respectful distance, and mutual appreciation for sweet berries.
* Serves as an unbreachable shoreline deterrent to river pirates. Playful, loves mackerel, wrestles with Mac (who matches his weight effortlessly), and will later enjoy a cozy winter den with a patchwork blanket.

### **Wojkw ("Fox") — Red Fox**
* Sly, elegant, and opportunistic. Operates as the perimeter scout and barnyard pest-controller. Has a habit of curling around Mac’s neck like a living fur stole on chilly mornings.

### **Scarlet ("Scar") — Acadian Red Squirrel**
* *Tamiasciurus hudsonicus*. Feisty, vocal, eight ounces of concentrated territorial fury and cheek.
* **Maya’s Bra-Squatter Tenant (Later Arc):**
  * Decides that Maya’s generous cleavage and fleece-lined bra constitute the most prime, heated, five-star luxury real estate in the province.
  * Burrows into her collar, curls up behind the underwire against her skin, and rides around with his tiny red head and whiskered snout popped out adorably over her neckline.
  * Indignantly chitters at anyone who gets too close; acts as Maya's hair-trigger personal proximity alarm.
  * Establishes Maya's impressive physical proportions through natural, hilarious situational comedy rather than objectification.

### **Maya**
* Fierce, resilient, practical, well-endowed, athletic, and razor-sharp. Former mechanical engineer and track runner from Moncton who fled enslavement in Sussex.
* Arrives injured; Mac treats her with ash crutches, Fundy pain-absorbing stones, and herbal care.
* **The Relationship Dynamic — Sovereign Partners & Future Lovers (Slow-Burn Romance):**
  * **Strictly Non-Paternal:** Their bond is **never** father-daughter. They are two adult equals navigating a harsh post-collapse world.
  * **The Slow-Burn Romance:** Built on deep mutual respect, shared labor, raw emotional vulnerability, and an intense, understated physical attraction.
  * **Mac's Nature:** Mac is a timeless, rugged *Kinap*—ageless, weathered, profoundly grounded, possessing a calm, masculine gravitas, dry wit, and quiet gentleness.
  * **The Intimacy & Electric Tension:** Living together in the intimate isolation of the headland lighthouse, their physical closeness is charged with subtle electricity—Mac's calloused hands guiding her hips and stance at the forge and on the sparring floor, tending each other's injuries, standing shoulder-to-shoulder in the high lantern room, and sharing the deep warmth of the stone hearth.
  * When Maya exhausts her anger against his chest, collapsing into him, it is not a child crying to a father—it is a proud, fierce woman finally lowering her guard and collapsing into the arms of the one man strong enough to hold her entire world, the man she is falling deeply in love with.
* Becomes Mac’s co-defender of the valley, student in animal communion, and the reluctant, amused landlord of Scar the squirrel.

## 4. Crafting, Teaching, and Practical Engineering (Core Narrative Pillar)

### The Philosophy of Craft & Mentorship
* **The Master and the Engineer:** Mac is an ancient craftsman who understands the marrow of physical matter; Maya is a trained mechanical engineer (Dalhousie) who understands the mathematics, vectors, and physics of forces. Their collaboration is a masterclass in grounded, tactile worldbuilding.
* **Crafting in Stages:** Every tool, weapon, structure, and survival item is built step by step in front of Maya, with her actively participating in the labor (pumping bellows, shaping handles, pressure-flaking stone, saddle-stitching leather).
* **The Pedagogical Dynamic:** Mac does not lecture; he teaches through tactile demonstration, asking probing questions, and having Maya feel the balance, color, grain, and temperature of materials.

### The Impossible Observed (Kinap Biology vs. Engineering Limits)
* **The Dalhousie Calculations:** Maya constantly calculates the mechanical limits of biological tissue (bone compressive strength, moment arms, torque on lumbar vertebrae, kinetic energy of impact).
* **Defying Biological Failure:**
  * Lifting the 837-lb *Krupp Essen 1912* naval armor block without spinal collapse (absorbing 15,000 in-lbs of torque into the pelvic arch).
  * Swinging the 32-lb sintered tungsten-carbide sledge at 80 bpm with zero lactic fatigue or ulnar collateral ligament shear (generating 2,400 Joules of kinetic force per strike).
  * Radiant heat immunity and cold-weld compression with zero scale inclusions.
* **Mac's Core Truth:** Mac needs no weapons for himself. His body is the weapon; his skeleton is the anvil; the bedrock is the hammer. Every weapon he creates is solely to balance the scales for Maya's mortal frame.

### The Combat Arsenal & The Four-Tier Doctrine
1. **The Seasoned White Ash Staff (Five Feet Two Inches):**
   * *Role:* Kinetic distance control, sweeping legs, shattering kneecaps, holding doorways and thresholds. Owns the ground six feet out.
2. **The 320-Layer Damascus Drop-Point Fighter (Six and a Half Inches):**
   * *Composition:* 1910 Nicholson farrier's rasp (1095 crucible carbon steel) + locomotive leaf spring (5160 Cr-Mn steel) + Swedish charcoal iron.
   * *Features:* 320 micro-layers, differential clay jacket (*hamon* temper line), glass-hard 63 HRC edge, tough shock-absorbing spine. Contoured heritage applewood handle scaled to Maya's hand arches; cold-peened copper bus-bar rivets; wet-formed, hot-waxed (*cuir bouilli*) vegetable-tanned sheath with copper welt, worn at a 20-degree forward cant.
   * *Role:* Close-quarters grappling, cutting muscle and tendon cables, clearing grapples, carving through heavy wool.
3. **The Molecular Razor / Obsidian Neck Scalpel (Three Inches):**
   * *Composition:* Ancient Pacific Northwest volcanic glass (*Kekwet*), conchoidal fracture via moose-antler billet and copper pressure flaker.
   * *Features:* 3-nanometer edge (30 Angstroms / single molecular chains of silica). Mounted in white cedar with charcoal-bone tempered spruce pitch, clamped by boiled deer sinew and sealed in balsam fir rosin.
   * *Role:* Emergency hideout weapon worn flat over sternum. Slices through cellular walls without crushing nerve sheaths or encountering resistance.
4. **The Retreat Covenant:**
   * If outnumbered by three or four, or if heavy firearms speak from the treeline, Maya drops pride, refuses to die for bravado, and runs straight to Mac.
