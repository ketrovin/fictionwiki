## 1. Malcolm "Mac" MacCallum — The Kinap

### Core Nature & Spiritual Anatomy
* **Identity:** An ex-archivist, bibliophile, and scholar who retreated to the red silt banks of the Petitcodiac (*Petkotkweyak*) a decade before the collapse. He carries the quiet, ancient mantle of a **Kinap** (a Mi'kmaw mythical titan of immense, grounded power).
* **The Situational Strength (Unteachable):**
  * Mac is as strong as he *wants* or *needs* to be in any given moment.
  * There are no bulging muscles, no theatrical energy auras, no grunting wind-ups. Whether lifting a 12-lb bag of meal, an 800-lb granite hearthstone, or wedging a runaway spruce log against a rushing tidal bore, his body answers his will with calm, effortless poise.
  * It is entirely inborn and **cannot be taught** to anyone else. To observers, it looks strangely casual—as if gravity simply forgot to pull down on whatever Mac is holding.
* **The Quiet Lethality (How Dangerous Mac Is):**
  * When pushed or defending his home, animals, or family, Mac is terrifyingly, lethally dangerous.
  * He has no bloodlust or bravado; his lethality is cold, sovereign, and inevitable like a spring tide. He can catch a swinging axe by the blade, snap forged iron crowbars like brittle twigs, and neutralize multiple armed hostiles without breaking a sweat or raising his voice.
  * Combined with an intelligence network of every bird, wolf, and beast within ten miles, ambushing him is impossible.
* **Animal Communion (Teachable):**
  * True mutual comprehension. Mac speaks in voice, posture, and intent, and animals understand him completely. Conversely, he hears and comprehends their thoughts, instincts, territorial disputes, and perspectives.
  * **This can be taught.** It is a discipline of shedding deceit, tuning mental frequency, reading the grammar of breath and scent, and respecting *Netukulimk* (the sacred balance). He will eventually mentor Maya in this art.
* **Minor Energy Manipulation:**
  * Subtle, tactile movement of warmth and vitality.
  * Mac can coax warmth into frostbitten fingers, calm a racing heart with a touch to the chest, settle the heat of a stone hearth, or guide the cellular vitality of living wood to weave and heal.
* **Voice & Mind (Philology & Literary Wit):**
  * Deeply well-read. He peppers his thoughts and deadpan observations with etymological roots, Latin/Greek puns, and classical allusions (*kynikos* for Nipn, *robur* for oak timber, *skiouros* for squirrels, *Petkotkweyak* vs. *Petitcodiac*).
  * Devoted reader of Terry Pratchett (especially *Guards! Guards!* and the Boots Theory), Defoe, Roman agricultural manuals (Cato, Columella), and 19th-century Maritime trade logs.
  * His tone is warm, wry, self-amused, and thoroughly Maritime. Zero academic lecturing in dialogue—only dry, affectionate, unpretentious wit.

## 2. The 10-Year Homestead (Stealth & Utility)

Mac has spent ten patient years establishing this sanctuary on the high ridge overlooking the Petitcodiac bends near Belliveau Village. Nothing is built to show off; everything is disguised as natural growth or weathered Acadian ruins.

```
       [ FUNDY / PETITCODIAC RED MUDFLATS ]
                    |
          [ Camouflaged Tidal Weirs ]
     (Woven brush & stakes; catches shad/flounder)
                    |
        +-----------+-----------+
        |                       |
[ The Stone Lighthouse ]   [ The 2-Room Cabin ]
(Winter sanctuary,         (Spring-Autumn base,
 granite fortress,         low-slung cedar,
 spiral stair library)      deep thermal hearth)
        |                       |
        +-----------+-----------+
                    |
      [ Living-Wood Palisade Fence ]
  (Braided willow/cedar/hawthorn; self-healing,
   with heavy counterweighted tamarack gate)
                    |
        [ Elevated Bog Boardwalk ]
  (Split-cedar walkway: cattail starch,
   cloudberries/bakeapples, medicinal moss)
```

### A. The Two-Room Cabin (Spring to Autumn Base)
* **Construction:** Low-profile, hand-hewn hemlock frame clad in split cedar shakes weathered to coastal drift-gray. Sits nestled into the leeward bank of black spruce.
* **Room 1 (The Common Room & Hearth):** Massive fieldstone fireplace backed by a two-ton dry clay-and-river-stone thermal battery. Holds heat for days. Workbench, hanging dried herbs, salt-pork racks, hand tools on pegs, and deep cedar shelving.
* **Room 2 (Sleeping Quarters & Pantry):** Insulated with wild sheep's wool and cattail down. Double bunk frames, secondary recovery cot, airtight zinc grain and flour casks, tool chests, seed stores.
* **Stealth Chimney Baffle:** Flue directs smoke down through an underground stone-and-gravel trench, cooling and filtering soot so the exhaust disperses at tree-canopy level as thin, odorless coastal mist.

### B. The Living-Wood Fence & Tamarack Gate
* Using his minor energy manipulation over ten years, Mac trained living black willow, thorny hawthorn, and coastal white cedar saplings to cross-braid and fuse their trunks as they grew.
* **The Palisade:** An eight-foot-tall, living, rooted, self-repairing wall that bends before hurricane-force gales without snapping. Completely impassable to intruders or large predators.
* **The Gate:** Heavy, hand-planed tamarack (hackmatack) hung on greased forged iron strap hinges. Perfectly balanced on a counterweighted stone pivot; glides shut with a quiet, solid click.

### C. The Bog Boardwalk
* Elevated split-cedar stringer walkway winding into the acidic peat bog behind the homestead.
* Prevents sinking into waist-deep quagmire while providing year-round access to:
  * **Cattails (*Typha*):** Starch flour from rhizomes, early shoots as greens, fluffy down for quilts and insulation.
  * **Cloudberries (Bakeapples):** Rare amber bog gold, loaded with Vitamin C.
  * **Bog Cranberries & Labrador Tea:** Cold-weather greens and tonic infusions.
  * **Sphagnum Moss:** Sterile, highly absorbent natural wound dressing that never rots.

### D. The Tidal Weirs (The Silt Traps)
* Sturdy brush-and-hurdle weirs staked into the red mud flats where the 40-foot Fundy tide recedes twice a day.
* Built to resemble random drift jams from the river.
* On the dropping tide, funnel flounder, gaspereau, and shad into a stone-lined catching pool, offering fresh protein twice daily without hooks or nets.

### E. The Stone Lighthouse (Winter Fortress & Tower Library)
* **Status:** Legally deeded to Mac before the collapse; sits on the headland overlooking Shepody Bay and the river bends.
* **First-Tier Keeper's Apartment:**
  * Mac’s **winter home**. Built into the massive four-foot-thick dressed granite base of the tower.
  * Heavily shuttered iron embrasures, heavy cast-iron cooking range, and deep stone thermal mass. Completely immune to Fundy nor'easters, freezing spray, and river ice jams.
* **The Curving Stair Library & The Rare Book Vault:**
  * The interior spiral stone staircase leading up the tower is lined on the outer curving wall with custom-fitted seasoned timber bookshelves.
  * Hundreds of dry, preserved volumes: classical Latin/Greek texts, botanical encyclopedias, civil engineering folios, OED lexicons, and Terry Pratchett paperbacks.
  * **The Antiquarian Vault (Rare Books & Folios):**
    * Mac spent decades as an archivist and collector. His lighthouse preserves the last great antiquarian collection in eastern Canada, stored in zinc-lined cedar chests and dry stone niches:
      * **Samuel Johnson’s *Dictionary of the English Language* (1755 First Edition, 2 folio volumes in full calfskin).**
      * **Champlain’s *Voyages* (1613/1632 edition)** with original folding copperplate maps of the Bay of Fundy (*Baie Françoise*) and the red river mouths.
      * **1730s Acadian Dyke Journals:** Hand-scribed notes detailing *aboiteau* construction (one-way tidal wooden clapper valves) and salt-marsh engineering.
      * **Gerard’s *Herball* (1633)** and Vitruvius’s *De Architectura* (1567 Venice edition) on hydraulic lime mortar and arches.
      * **Newton's *Principia* (1726 edition).**
    * Using his subtle energy manipulation, Mac keeps the stairwell at a perfect, stable microclimate (45% humidity, zero mold or salt damp), ensuring these physical survivors of human civilization outlast the collapse by centuries.
* **Upper Lantern Gallery & Watchroom:**
  * 360-degree glassed lantern room with brass naval telescope. Unbroken 20-mile surveillance over river channels, highways, and forest lines. Optical shutter signal station.

## 3. The Animal Companions & Cast

### **Nipn ("Summer") — Eastern Timber Wolf**
* Massive, silver-grey wolf with amber eyes. Mac’s primary scout, hunting partner, and intellectual equal in wilderness logic.
* Highly dignified, observant, and possessing dry comedic timing in his communication with Mac. Tolerates small creatures with stoic patience.

### **The Fundy Bear — Shoreline Guard & Beggar Bear**
* 450-lb Atlantic black bear. Befriended by Mac through fish scraps, respectful distance, and mutual appreciation for sweet berries.
* Serves as an unbreachable shoreline deterrent to river pirates. Playful, loves mackerel, wrestles with Mac (who matches his weight effortlessly), and will later enjoy a cozy winter den with a patchwork blanket.

### **Wojkw ("Fox") — Red Fox**
* Sly, elegant, and opportunistic. Operates as the perimeter scout and barnyard pest-controller. Has a habit of curling around Mac’s neck like a living fur stole on chilly mornings.

### **Scarlet ("Scar") — Acadian Red Squirrel**
* *Tamiasciurus hudsonicus*. Feisty, vocal, eight ounces of concentrated territorial fury and cheek.
* **Maya’s Bra-Squatter Tenant (Later Arc):**
  * Decides that Maya’s generous cleavage and fleece-lined bra constitute the most prime, heated, five-star luxury real estate in the province.
  * Burrows into her collar, curls up behind the underwire against her skin, and rides around with his tiny red head and whiskered snout popped out adorably over her neckline.
  * Indignantly chitters at anyone who gets too close; acts as Maya's hair-trigger personal proximity alarm.
  * Establishes Maya's impressive physical proportions through natural, hilarious situational comedy rather than objectification.

### **Maya (Arriving Later in Book 1)**
* Fierce, resilient, practical, well-endowed, and weary from post-collapse road travel.
* Arrives injured; Mac treats her with ash crutches and Fundy pain-absorbing stones.
* Becomes Mac’s student in animal communication and homestead craft, his partner in defending the valley, and the reluctant landlord of Scar the squirrel.
